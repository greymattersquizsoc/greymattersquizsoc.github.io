CORE CURRICULUM

Foundational texts establishing an intellectual baseline.

BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
THINKING, FAST AND SLOW ↗
Daniel Kahneman
Deconstructs the dichotomy between intuitive, emotional thought (System 1) and deliberate, logical processing (System 2), exposing cognitive biases.
APPLIED MICROECONOMICS
FREAKONOMICS ↗
Steven D. Levitt & Stephen J. Dubner
Applies economic theory to unconventional, everyday subjects, proving that human behavior is fundamentally driven by a complex, often hidden web of incentives.
MACRO-HISTORY
SAPIENS ↗
Yuval Noah Harari
Examines the cognitive revolutions and shared cultural myths—such as currency and corporations—that allowed Homo sapiens to dominate the globe.
INSTITUTIONAL GEOPOLITICS
WHY NATIONS FAIL ↗
Daron Acemoglu & James Robinson
Provides a definitive framework proving that political and economic institutional design—not geography or culture—dictates the wealth and power of societies.
THEORETICAL PHYSICS
A BRIEF HISTORY OF TIME ↗
Stephen Hawking
A foundational overview of cosmology, exploring the architecture of the universe from the Big Bang and black holes to the search for a unified theory of everything.
COGNITIVE SCIENCE & LOGIC
GÖDEL, ESCHER, BACH ↗
Douglas Hofstadter
A Pulitzer-winning exploration of formal systems, artificial intelligence, and how meaning emerges from meaningless symbols through self-referential loops.
SYSTEMS & INFORMATION
THE INFORMATION ↗
James Gleick
Traces the evolution of human communication from African talking drums to quantum computing, illustrating how data engineered modern civilization.
EVOLUTIONARY BIOLOGY
THE SELFISH GENE ↗
Richard Dawkins
Shifts the perspective of evolution from the organism to the gene, introducing the concept of the "meme" to explain how cultural ideas replicate and mutate.
SPATIAL POLITICS
PRISONERS OF GEOGRAPHY ↗
Tim Marshall
Analyzes how the physical environment—mountains, rivers, and oceans—irrevocably constrains the foreign policy and economic leverage of world powers.
VISUAL LITERACY & ART
WAYS OF SEEING ↗
John Berger
Deconstructs the traditional aesthetics of Western art, analyzing how socio-economic structures, gender, and power dictate visual representation and perception.
