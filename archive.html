A curated monthly ledger of exceptional reading across all disciplines.

[ CURATION PHILOSOPHY ]

To master the art of lateral quizzing, one must read widely and indiscriminately. Each month, the Society curates two of the most fascinating essays, articles, and research papers available on the internet. From evolutionary game theory and linguistics to neurology and art history, this archive directs our cohort to the ideas shaping human knowledge. All links route directly to the original authors and publications.
