SOCIETY ROSTER

A decentralized intellectual collective driven by consensus and shared inquiry.

[ INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK ]

Grey Matters is founded upon a culture of collaborative inquiry and intellectual egalitarianism. Structural designations within the society exist to curate competitive excellence, design academic frameworks, and provide structured mentorship. We operate as a unified collective where ideas are evaluated strictly on their merit, and discourse flows freely across all cohorts. The strength of the institution relies on this shared pursuit of knowledge, ensuring every member contributes to the collective intelligence of the society.

[ EXECUTIVE ]

PRESIDENT

The primary steward of the society's academic direction. The President fosters a culture of rigorous inquiry, coordinates the society’s delegations for external quizzing circuits, and ensures that all internal initiatives align with a shared standard of competitive excellence.

[ EXECUTIVE ]

VICE PRESIDENT

The principal architect of the society's internal frameworks and event mechanics. Working in tandem with the broader cohort, the Vice President structures comprehensive training modules and designs innovative competition formats to continuously challenge and elevate the team's lateral thinking.

[ SENIOR COHORT ]

CORE MEMBERS

The society's senior quizzing cohort and domain specialists. Core Members take an active role in shaping the intellectual landscape of the club by curating high-quality question banks, leading school teams in high stakes competitions, and providing dedicated mentorship to newer members.

[ GENERAL COHORT ]

MEMBERS

The established competitive foundation of the society. Members actively represent the institution across inter-school quizzing circuits, engage in peer-to-peer knowledge sharing that keep the society's collective knowledge sharp and adaptable.

[ INDUCTEES ]

TRAINEES

The society's incoming generation of quizzers. Trainees are immersed in a collaborative learning environment where they develop foundational research skills, practice decoding complex queries, and work closely with senior members to prepare for active tournament participation.
